Synthesizing empirical studies and industry security standards.

Empirical Adoption

Analysis of the Adoption of Security Headers in HTTP

Buchanan et al. (2018) established the critical adoption gap in Top 1M sites. Found that despite low complexity, essential headers like HSTS and CSP were neglected by a majority of highly-trafficked endpoints.
Transport Standards

NIST SP 800-52 Rev. 2: TLS Guidelines

Standardizes the transition to TLS 1.2 and 1.3. Defines the necessity of FIPS-based cipher suites and certificate transparency as the baseline for trusted organizational communication.
Defensive Best Practice

OWASP SSRF & Headers Prevention Guides

Defines the zero-trust boundary for scanner implementations. Emphasizes DNS pre-resolution, private IP blocking, and redirection boundary enforcement as mandatory safeguards.

"The results show that, while implementation is increasing, it is still not implemented on many of the top sites... a distinct effort is required to achieve a passing grade." — Buchanan, IET Information Security (2018)

Sources: IET InfoSec (2018), NIST (2019), OWASP Cheat Sheets
