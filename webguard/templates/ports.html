Non-Aggressive Port Probe Methodology

WeeShark adheres strictly to benign, non-destructive network assessments:

  • Probing is restricted strictly to 5 standard ports (80, 443, 22, 21, 8080).
  • Socket handshakes use a single lightweight SYN attempt with an automated 1.2-second timeout.
  • No aggressive vulnerability fuzzing, exploit payloads, or volumetric scanning is ever performed.
