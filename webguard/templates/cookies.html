Secure Flag

Instructs the browser that the cookie should only be transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, preventing interception on untrusted Wi-Fi or networks.

HttpOnly Flag

Blocks client-side scripts (document.cookie) from accessing the cookie, preventing session token theft in the event of a Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) vulnerability.

SameSite Flag

Controls whether cookies are sent with cross-site requests. Setting SameSite=Lax or Strict defends against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks.
